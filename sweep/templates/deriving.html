What Sweep is preparing

  • Your experienceEmployers, titles and dates, read from the document rather than guessed.
  • Skills and technologiesEverything the résumé names, collapsed so one technology counts once however many ways you spell it.
  • Which of them matter mostWeighted by what the document shows you actually built with, not by how often the word appears.
  • Your search profileThe job titles to search and the weights that will rank what comes back.

All of it happens in one pass, so there are no steps to tick off — the screen moves on when the whole profile is ready.
